Example 2 — "My employer is not paying salary for 3 months."
## Issue Category
Employment / Labour Dispute
## Facts Identified
- Employer-employee relationship exists
- Salary withheld for approximately 3 months
## Applicable Laws
- Payment of Wages Act, 1936
- Industrial Disputes Act, 1947
- Code on Wages, 2019
## Relevant Sections
- Payment of Wages Act §4 & §5 (fixation and time of payment of wages)
- Code on Wages §17 (time limit for payment)
## Evidence Required
- Appointment / offer letter
- Payslips and salary structure
- Bank statements showing missed credits
- Email or written communication with employer
## Recommended Action
1. Send a formal written demand / legal notice to the employer.
2. File a complaint with the Labour Commissioner having jurisdiction.
3. If unresolved, approach the Labour Court / appropriate industrial tribunal.
## Disclaimer
This is informational legal guidance only and is not a substitute for professional legal advice. Please consult a qualified advocate for your specific case.
